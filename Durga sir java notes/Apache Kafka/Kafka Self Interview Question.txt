
# Kafka Interview Questions & Answers (Basic to Intermediate)

## Q1. Kafka kya hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka ek **distributed event streaming platform** hai jo real-time me data ko **publish (produce)** aur **consume** karne ke liye use hota hai.

Iska use high throughput, fault tolerance aur scalable messaging ke liye hota hai.

**One Line Interview Answer**

> Kafka is a distributed event streaming platform used for building real-time data pipelines and event-driven applications.

---

=================================================================================
1. High Throughput kya hota hai?

Simple Meaning:
Ek second me bahut saare messages process karne ki capability.

Real-life Example

Maan lo Flipkart ki Big Billion Days sale chal rahi hai.

Ek second me:

1 lakh orders aaye
50,000 payments aaye
20,000 notifications bhejne hain

Agar normal application hai to slow ho sakti hai.

Lekin Kafka ek second me lakhs ya millions of messages handle kar sakta hai.

Interview Answer:

High Throughput means Kafka can process a very large number of messages in a very short time.

2. Fault Tolerance kya hota hai?

Simple Meaning:
Agar system ka koi part fail ho jaye, tab bhi application chalti rahe aur data loss na ho.

Example

Maan lo tumhare paas 3 Kafka Brokers hain.

Broker-1
Broker-2
Broker-3

Aur Broker-1 achanak band ho gaya.

Agar replication enabled hai to Broker-2 ya Broker-3 data serve karte rahenge.

User ko pata bhi nahi chalega ki ek broker down ho gaya.

Isko kehte hain Fault Tolerance.

Interview Answer:

Fault Tolerance means Kafka continues working even if a broker fails, without losing data (when replication is configured).

3. Scalable Messaging kya hota hai?

Simple Meaning:
Jab users badh jaye to system ko easily bada (scale) kiya ja sake.

Example

Aaj:

1 Broker
1000 Messages/sec

Kal traffic badh gaya.

Ab tum aur brokers add kar sakte ho.

Broker-1
Broker-2
Broker-3
Broker-4

Ab Kafka aur jyada messages handle karega.

Isko Scalability kehte hain.

Interview Answer:

Scalability means Kafka can handle increasing traffic by adding more brokers and partitions.

* Live Tracking Applications

## Q3. Kafka ke main components kaun kaun se hain?

* Producer
* Consumer

## Q4. Producer kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Producer wo application hoti hai jo Kafka Topic me messages bhejti hai.

**Example**

Order Service → Kafka Topic

---

## Q5. Consumer kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Consumer wo application hoti hai jo Kafka Topic se messages read karti hai.

**Example**

Inventory Service Order Topic se message read karti hai.

---

## Q6. Topic kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Topic ek logical channel hai jisme Producer messages bhejta hai aur Consumer wahi se messages read karta hai.

Example:

```
orders
payments
notifications
```

---

## Q7. Partition kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Topic ko multiple parts me divide kiya jata hai jise Partition kehte hain.

Benefits

* Parallel Processing
* High Throughput
* Scalability

---

## Q8. Broker kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Broker Kafka Server hota hai jo messages ko store karta hai.

Ek Kafka Cluster me multiple brokers ho sakte hain.

---

## Q9. Consumer Group kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Consumer Group ek group hota hai jisme multiple consumers milkar partitions ko consume karte hain.

Har partition ek time par sirf ek consumer hi consume karta hai.

---

## Q10. Kafka me message flow kaise hota hai?

**Answer**

```
Producer
     |
     V
 Topic
     |
Partition
     |

     |

```

---

## Q11. Topic create kaise karte hain?

**Answer**

```bash
kafka-topics.bat --create --topic my-first-topic --bootstrap-server localhost:9092 --partitions 1 --replication-factor 1
```

---

## Q12. Producer kaise start karte hain?

**Answer**

```bash
kafka-console-producer.bat --broker-list localhost:9092 --topic my-first-topic
```

## Q13. Consumer kaise start karte hain?

**Answer**


## Q14. --from-beginning ka matlab kya hota hai?

Consumer topic ke saare purane messages bhi read karega.

Agar ye option nahi denge to sirf naye messages read honge.

# ZooKeeper Interview Questions

---

## Q15. ZooKeeper kya hai?

**Answer**

ZooKeeper Kafka ka coordination service tha jo multiple brokers ko manage karta tha.

Ye Leader Election, Metadata Management aur Cluster Coordination karta tha.

---

## Q16. ZooKeeper ke main responsibilities kya thi?

**Answer**

* Leader Election
* Broker Health Monitoring
* Metadata Store
* Topic Management
* Distributed Locking

---

## Q17. Leader Election kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Har partition ka ek leader hota hai.

ZooKeeper decide karta tha

* Kaun leader hoga
* Kaun follower hoga
* Leader fail ho jaye to naya leader kaun banega

---

## Q18. Broker Health Monitoring kya hota hai?

**Answer**

ZooKeeper brokers ki heartbeat monitor karta tha.

Heartbeat missing hone par broker ko dead mark karta tha aur naya leader select karta tha.

---

## Q19. ZooKeeper metadata me kya store karta tha?

**Answer**

* Broker Information
* Topic Information
* Partition Details
* Replication Information
* ISR List

---

## Q20. ZooKeeper me heartbeat kya hoti hai?

**Answer**

Heartbeat ek periodic signal hota hai jo broker ZooKeeper ko bhejta hai.

Agar heartbeat nahi aaye to broker dead mana jata hai.

---

## Q21. Distributed Locking kya hota hai?

**Answer**

Distributed Locking ensure karta hai ki ek hi resource ko ek time par sirf ek broker modify kare.

Isse data corruption nahi hota.

---

## Q22. ZooKeeper ko Kafka se kyun remove kiya gaya?

**Answer**

Reasons

* Performance Bottleneck
* Complex Architecture
* Extra Component
* More Network Calls
* Cloud Scalability Issues

---

## Q23. KRaft kya hai?

**Answer**

KRaft (Kafka Raft Metadata Mode) Kafka ka naya metadata management system hai jo ZooKeeper ki jagah use hota hai.

---

## Q24. KRaft kya kya manage karta hai?

**Answer**

* Leader Election
* Metadata Management
* Topic Management
* Broker Monitoring

Sab kuch Kafka ke andar hi hota hai.

---

## Q25. Kya latest Kafka me ZooKeeper required hai?

**Answer**

Nahi.

Kafka 3.x versions me KRaft use hota hai aur ZooKeeper optional/deprecated hai.

---

# Kafka Interview Questions & Answers (Part-2)

## Q31. Kafka Broker kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka Broker ek server hota hai jisme Kafka install hota hai. Ye producer se messages receive karta hai, unhe disk par store karta hai aur consumers ko serve karta hai.

**Interview One-Liner:**

> A Kafka Broker is a Kafka server responsible for storing and serving messages.

---

## Q32. Producer aur Consumer direct communicate kyun nahi karte?

**Answer:**

Producer aur Consumer ke beech Kafka Broker hota hai.

Iske benefits:

* Producer ko Consumer ki information nahi chahiye.
* Consumer kabhi bhi baad me messages read kar sakta hai.
* System loosely coupled rehta hai.
* High traffic ko easily handle kiya ja sakta hai.

---

## Q33. Kafka asynchronous communication kaise provide karta hai?

**Answer:**

Producer message ko Kafka Topic me store kar deta hai aur turant apna kaam continue karta hai.

Consumer baad me apni speed se message read karta hai.

Dono ko ek dusre ka wait nahi karna padta.

---

## Q34. Kafka me Pull Model kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka me Consumer khud Broker se message request karta hai.

Broker automatically message push nahi karta.

**Example**

Consumer bolta hai:

> "Mujhe naye messages do."

Broker messages return kar deta hai.

---

## Q35. Kafka Pull Model kyun use karta hai?

**Answer:**

Pull Model ke advantages:

* Consumer apni speed se data read karta hai.
* Slow consumer par overload nahi hota.
* Better scalability milti hai.
* Consumer apna offset manage kar sakta hai.

---

## Q36. RabbitMQ aur Kafka me message delivery ka difference kya hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka generally **Pull Model** use karta hai.

RabbitMQ mostly **Push Model** use karta hai.

**Interview One-Liner:**

> Kafka consumers pull messages, whereas RabbitMQ typically pushes messages to consumers.

---

## Q37. Kafka Cluster kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Multiple Kafka Brokers milkar Kafka Cluster banate hain.

Cluster ka purpose:

* High Availability
* Load Distribution
* Fault Tolerance
* Scalability

---

## Q38. Kafka Cluster ki zarurat kyun padti hai?

**Answer:**

Single Broker ki limitation hoti hai.

Cluster use karne se:

* Load distribute hota hai.
* Data replicate hota hai.
* Broker failure ke baad bhi application chalti rehti hai.
* Performance improve hoti hai.

---

## Q39. High Availability kya hoti hai?

**Answer:**

High Availability ka matlab hai ki agar ek Broker fail ho jaye to bhi system continuously available rahe.

Users ko service interruption na ho.

---

## Q40. Kafka by default kitne Brokers ke saath start hota hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka by default sirf **1 Broker** ke saath start hota hai.

Learning aur development ke liye ye sufficient hota hai.

---

## Q41. Kafka me default Broker ID kya hoti hai?

**Answer:**

Default configuration me Broker ID hoti hai:

```properties
broker.id=0
```

Har Broker ki ID unique honi chahiye.

---

## Q42. Kafka me default configuration kya hoti hai?

**Answer:**

Default setup:

* 1 Broker
* 1 Partition
* Replication Factor = 1

Ye local development ke liye hota hai.

---

## Q43. Kafka me Cluster kaise banaya jata hai?

**Answer:**

Cluster banane ke liye multiple Kafka Brokers start kiye jate hain.

Har Broker ka:

* Broker ID alag hota hai.
* Configuration alag hoti hai.
* Port alag hota hai.

Sab Brokers milkar Cluster banate hain.

---

## Q44. Single Broker aur Kafka Cluster me kya difference hai?

| Single Broker       | Kafka Cluster     |
| ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Sirf ek server      | Multiple servers  |
| Low availability    | High availability |
| Development         | Production        |
| Limited scalability | Highly scalable   |

---

## Q45. Local machine par Kafka me sirf ek Broker kyun use kiya jata hai?

**Answer:**

Kyuki:

* Setup simple hota hai.
* Kam RAM aur CPU lagta hai.
* Learning aur testing ke liye enough hota hai.
* Cluster configuration ki zarurat nahi hoti.

---

## Q46. Production environment me generally kitne Brokers use hote hain?

**Answer:**

Production me commonly:

* 3 Brokers
* 5 Brokers
* 7 Brokers

Use kiye jate hain.

Odd number Brokers rakhna leader election aur reliability ke liye common practice hai.

---

## Q47. Bootstrap Server kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Bootstrap Server Kafka Cluster ka entry point hota hai.

Producer aur Consumer sabse pehle isi Broker se connect karte hain.

Uske baad Kafka Cluster ki metadata information mil jati hai.

**Interview One-Liner:**

> Bootstrap Server is the initial Kafka Broker that clients use to connect to the Kafka cluster.

---

## Q48. Kya Bootstrap Server poora Kafka Cluster hota hai?

**Answer:**

Nahi.

Bootstrap Server sirf initial connection ke liye hota hai.

Connection establish hone ke baad client ko Cluster ke baaki Brokers ki information mil jati hai.

---

## Q49. Bootstrap Server me localhost:9092 ka kya matlab hai?

**Answer:**

* localhost → Kafka local machine par chal raha hai.
* 9092 → Kafka Broker ka default port.

---

## Q50. Agar Bootstrap Server down ho jaye to kya hoga?

**Answer:**

Agar client ke paas Cluster metadata already hai to wo dusre Brokers se communicate kar sakta hai.

Lekin naye clients Bootstrap Server se connect nahi kar paayenge jab tak koi available Bootstrap Broker na ho.

# Kafka Interview Questions & Answers (Part-3)

## Q51. Kya Topic messages ko directly store karta hai?

**Answer:**

Nahi.

Topic ek logical entity hai. Messages actually **Partitions** ke andar store hote hain.

**Interview One-Liner:**

> A Kafka Topic is a logical abstraction, while the actual data is stored inside partitions.

---

## Q52. Kafka me Partitions ki zarurat kyun padti hai?

**Answer:**

Agar saare messages ek hi jagah store honge to performance aur storage dono par load badh jayega.

Isliye Kafka Topic ko multiple Partitions me divide karta hai.

Benefits:

* Better Performance
* Parallel Processing
* Load Distribution
* Scalability

---

## Q53. Real-life example se Partition samjhao.

**Answer:**

Suppose Indian Railways ki 200 trains har 30 seconds me GPS location bhej rahi hain.

Agar sabhi messages ek hi Partition me jayenge to bottleneck create hoga.

Kafka Topic ko multiple Partitions me divide karta hai jisse load distribute ho jata hai.

---

## Q54. Partition ka main purpose kya hai?

**Answer:**

Partition ka purpose hai:

* Topic ko multiple parts me divide karna.
* Parallel message processing karna.
* Storage load distribute karna.
* Scalability improve karna.

---

## Q55. Kya ek Topic ke saare Partitions same Broker me hote hain?

**Answer:**

Nahi.

Production environment me Partitions alag-alag Brokers par distribute kiye ja sakte hain.

Isse system ki performance aur availability improve hoti hai.

---

## Q56. Partitions ko different Brokers me distribute karne ka benefit kya hai?

**Answer:**

Benefits:

* Load Balancing
* High Availability
* Better Performance
* Scalability
* Fault Tolerance

---

## Q57. Multi-Broker Partition Distribution kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Ek Topic ke different Partitions alag Brokers par store kiye ja sakte hain.

Example:

* Partition 0 → Broker 1
* Partition 1 → Broker 2
* Partition 2 → Broker 3
* Partition 3 → Broker 4

Isse workload distribute ho jata hai.

---

## Q58. Kafka server start karte waqt meta.properties missing error kyun aata hai?

**Answer:**

Ye error tab aata hai jab Kafka ka storage directory delete ya clean ho jata hai.

Kafka ko Cluster Metadata ke liye **meta.properties** file chahiye hoti hai.

---

## Q59. meta.properties file ka purpose kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

meta.properties file Kafka ke storage ki metadata information store karti hai.

Isme Cluster ID aur Broker ki important information hoti hai.

---

## Q60. Kafka me Cluster ID kya hoti hai?

**Answer:**

Cluster ID ek unique identifier hota hai jo Kafka Cluster ko identify karta hai.

Har Kafka Cluster ki apni unique Cluster ID hoti hai.

---

## Q61. Kafka me Cluster ID kaise generate karte hain?

**Answer:**

Command:

```bash
kafka-storage.sh random-uuid
```

Ye ek unique Cluster ID generate karta hai.

---

## Q62. Kafka Storage Format command ka use kya hai?

**Answer:**

Command:

```bash
kafka-storage.sh format -t <cluster-id> -c server.properties
```

Ye Kafka Storage ko initialize karta hai aur required metadata files create karta hai.

---

## Q63. Kafka Topic create karne par default kitne Partitions bante hain?

**Answer:**

Agar Partition specify nahi karte to default **1 Partition** create hota hai.

---

## Q64. Multiple Partitions ke saath Topic kaise create karte hain?

**Answer:**

Example:

```bash
kafka-topics.sh --create --topic orders --partitions 3 --bootstrap-server localhost:9092
```

Ye Topic ko 3 Partitions ke saath create karega.

---

## Q65. Kafka me Topic Describe command ka use kya hai?

**Answer:**

Command:

```bash
kafka-topics.sh --describe --topic orders --bootstrap-server localhost:9092
```

Ye Topic ki complete information show karti hai.

Jaise:

* Partition Count
* Replication Factor
* Leader
* Replicas
* ISR

---

## Q66. TopicId kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka har Topic ko internally ek unique Topic ID assign karta hai.

Ye internal identification ke liye use hoti hai.

---

## Q67. Topic Description me PartitionCount ka kya matlab hota hai?

**Answer:**

PartitionCount batata hai ki Topic me total kitne Partitions hain.

Example:

```
PartitionCount: 3
```

Matlab Topic me 3 Partitions hain.

---

## Q68. Topic Description me ReplicationFactor ka kya matlab hota hai?

**Answer:**

Replication Factor batata hai ki har Partition ki kitni copies maintain ki ja rahi hain.

Example:

```
ReplicationFactor: 3
```

Matlab har Partition ki 3 copies hongi.

---

## Q69. Topic Description me Configs kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Configs Topic ki configuration settings hoti hain.

Example:

```
segment.bytes=1073741824
```

Iska matlab ek log segment file ka maximum size 1 GB hai.

---

## Q70. segment.bytes configuration ka purpose kya hai?

**Answer:**

Ye define karta hai ki Kafka ek log segment file ko maximum kitne size tak store karega.

Default value approximately **1 GB** hoti hai.

---

## Q71. Topic Description me Leader field kya batati hai?

**Answer:**

Leader field batati hai ki kisi Partition ka Leader Broker kaunsa hai.

Producer aur Consumer Leader Broker ke through hi communicate karte hain.

---

## Q72. Topic Description me Replicas field kya batati hai?

**Answer:**

Replicas field batati hai ki kisi Partition ki copies kin Brokers par available hain.

---

## Q73. Topic Description me ISR kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

ISR ka full form hai **In-Sync Replicas**.

Ye un Replicas ki list hoti hai jo Leader ke saath fully synchronized hain.

---

## Q74. Agar Replication Factor = 1 ho to Replicas aur ISR me kya dikhega?

**Answer:**

Agar Replication Factor 1 hai to:

* Sirf ek Replica hoga.
* Wahi Leader hoga.
* Wahi ISR me bhi hoga.

Example:

```
Leader : 1001
Replicas : 1001
ISR : 1001
```

---

## Q75. Agar Replication Factor = 1 ho aur Broker down ho jaye to kya hoga?

**Answer:**

Partition unavailable ho jayega.

Kyuki backup Replica available nahi hoga.

Isi wajah se Production me generally Replication Factor 3 rakha jata hai.

# Kafka Interview Questions & Answers (Part-4)

## Q76. Kafka Consumer me partition information kaise dekh sakte hain?

**Answer:**

Consumer run karte waqt ye properties use karte hain:

```bash
--property print.partition=true --property print.value=true
```

Isse har message ke saath uska Partition Number bhi print hota hai.

---

## Q77. `print.partition=true` property ka use kya hai?

**Answer:**

Ye property batati hai ki message kis Partition se read hua hai.

Interview me debugging aur verification ke liye iska use hota hai.

---

## Q78. `print.value=true` property ka use kya hai?

**Answer:**

Ye actual message value ko console par print karti hai.

Agar ye property na ho to sirf metadata dikh sakta hai.

---

## Q79. Kafka me saare messages ek hi Partition me kyun chale jaate hain?

**Answer:**

Kyuki Kafka ka default partitioner **Sticky Partitioner** hota hai.

Ye kuch time ya data threshold tak messages ko ek hi Partition me bhejta hai, phir doosre Partition par switch karta hai.

---

## Q80. Sticky Partitioner kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Sticky Partitioner Kafka ka default partitioning strategy hai.

Ye consecutive messages ko temporarily ek hi Partition me bhejta hai taaki batching improve ho aur performance better mile.

**Interview One-Liner:**

> Sticky Partitioner improves batching by sending consecutive messages to the same partition for a short period.

---

## Q81. Kafka Sticky Partitioner ka benefit kya hai?

**Answer:**

Benefits:

* Better Batch Processing
* Network Calls kam hoti hain.
* Producer Performance improve hoti hai.
* Throughput increase hota hai.

---

## Q82. Round Robin Partitioner kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Round Robin Partitioner messages ko sequentially sabhi Partitions me equally distribute karta hai.

Example:

* Message 1 → Partition 0
* Message 2 → Partition 1
* Message 3 → Partition 2
* Message 4 → Partition 0

---

## Q83. Round Robin Partitioner ka use kab karte hain?

**Answer:**

Jab hume sabhi Partitions me equal load distribution chahiye ho.

Ye workload ko evenly spread karta hai.

---

## Q84. Round Robin Partitioner kaise configure karte hain?

**Answer:**

Producer start karte waqt ye property pass karte hain:

```bash
--producer-property partitioner.class=org.apache.kafka.clients.producer.RoundRobinPartitioner
```

---

## Q85. Sticky Partitioner aur Round Robin Partitioner me difference kya hai?

| Sticky Partitioner                                  | Round Robin Partitioner                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Default Partitioner                                 | Optional Configuration                      |
| Same Partition me kuch time tak messages bhejta hai | Har message ko next Partition me bhejta hai |
| Better Batching                                     | Equal Distribution                          |
| Performance optimized                               | Load balancing optimized                    |

---

## Q86. Kaise verify karoge ki Round Robin Partitioner properly kaam kar raha hai?

**Answer:**

Consumer me Partition print enable karenge.

Agar messages alag-alag Partitions se aa rahe hain to Round Robin correctly kaam kar raha hai.

---

## Q87. Kafka me message kis Partition me gaya, ye kaise check karte hain?

**Answer:**

Consumer run karte waqt:

```bash
--property print.partition=true
```

Isse har message ke saath uska Partition Number display hota hai.

# Kafka Interview Questions & Answers (Part-5)

## Q101. Kafka Cluster me Load Distribution kya hota hai?

## Q102. Kafka me Load Distribution Broker level par hota hai ya Partition level par?

Producer messages ko Partitions me bhejta hai aur Partitions alag-alag Brokers par distribute hote hain.

## Q103. Kafka Partitions ko multiple Brokers par kyun distribute karta hai?

* Better Performance
* High Availability

* Fault Tolerance

## Q104. Ek Topic ke Partitions Brokers par kaise distribute hote hain?

**Answer:**

* Partition 2 → Broker 3

Isse har Broker apne assigned Partition ka load handle karta hai.

## Q105. Kafka Producer message kis Partition me bhejta hai?

* Producer ki configuration kya hai.

## Q106. Kafka Producer Partition ka selection kaise karta hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka Producer mainly 3 tarike se Partition select karta hai:

1. Key-based Partitioning
2. Round Robin / Sticky Partitioner (configuration ke hisab se)
3. Custom Partitioner

---

## Q107. Key-based Partitioning kya hoti hai?

**Answer:**

Agar Producer message ke saath Key bhejta hai to Kafka us Key ke basis par Partition decide karta hai.

Isse same Key ke saare messages hamesha same Partition me jate hain.

---

## Q108. Kafka me Key-based Partitioning ka formula kya hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka generally ye formula use karta hai:

```text
partition = hash(key) % number_of_partitions
```

Ye hash value ke basis par target Partition select karta hai.

---

## Q109. Same Key ke messages hamesha same Partition me kyun jate hain?

**Answer:**

Kyuki Kafka Key ka hash calculate karta hai.

Same Key ka hash same hota hai, isliye Partition bhi same select hota hai.

---

## Q110. Same Key ko same Partition me bhejne ka benefit kya hai?

**Answer:**

Sabse bada benefit **Message Ordering** hai.

Ek hi Key ke messages sequence me process hote hain.

Example:

Customer-101 ke saare Order Events ek hi Partition me rahenge.

---

## Q111. Key-based Partitioning ka real-life example bataiye.

**Answer:**

Suppose Banking Application hai.

Account Number = Key

Account Number 1001 ke saare Transactions ek hi Partition me jayenge.

Isse transaction order maintain rahega.

---

## Q112. Agar Producer Key nahi bheje to Partition kaise select hota hai?

**Answer:**

Agar Key nahi di gayi hai to Producer configured partitioning strategy ke according Partition select karta hai.

Jaise Round Robin ya Sticky Partitioner.

---

## Q113. Custom Partitioner kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Custom Partitioner me developer apna khud ka Partition selection logic likhta hai.

Ye special business requirements ke liye use hota hai.

## Q114. Custom Partitioner kab use kiya jata hai?

**Answer:**

Jab business rule ke hisab se messages ko specific Partitions me bhejna ho.

Example:

* VIP Customers
* Premium Orders
* Region-wise Data

---

## Q115. Kafka me Leader Partition kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Har Partition ka ek Leader hota hai.

Producer aur Consumer dono Leader ke saath communicate karte hain.

Leader hi Read aur Write operations handle karta hai.

---

## Q116. Follower Replica ka role kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Follower Replica Leader ke data ki copy maintain karta hai.

Ye backup ke liye hota hai aur normally Read/Write requests handle nahi karta.

---

## Q117. Kafka me Producer aur Consumer kis Replica se communicate karte hain?

**Answer:**

Producer aur Consumer hamesha **Leader Replica** ke saath communicate karte hain.

Follower Replica directly requests serve nahi karta.

---

## Q118. Leader Replica ki responsibilities kya hoti hain?

**Answer:**

Leader Replica:

* Producer se messages receive karta hai.
* Consumers ko messages serve karta hai.
* Followers ko data replicate karta hai.

---

## Q119. Follower Replica ki responsibilities kya hoti hain?

**Answer:**

Follower Replica:

* Leader se data copy karta hai.
* Backup maintain karta hai.
* Failure hone par Leader ban sakta hai.

---

## Q120. Agar Leader Broker fail ho jaye to kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka automatically ek healthy Follower ko naya Leader bana deta hai.

Producer aur Consumer us naye Leader se communicate karna start kar dete hain.

---

## Q121. Broker Failure ke baad Kafka service kaise continue rehti hai?

**Answer:**

Replication ki wajah se.

Leader fail hone par Follower Leader ban jata hai.

Isse downtime minimum hota hai.

---

## Q122. Kafka me Automatic Leader Election kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Leader fail hone par Kafka automatically ISR me available Follower ko Leader promote kar deta hai.

Is process ko Leader Election kehte hain.

---

## Q123. Load Redistribution kya hota hai?

**Answer:**

Broker failure ke baad jis Broker par naya Leader banta hai, us Broker par traffic shift ho jata hai.

Is process ko Load Redistribution kehte hain.

---

## Q124. Kafka me Message Ordering kab guarantee hoti hai?

**Answer:**

Kafka sirf **same Partition** ke andar Message Ordering guarantee karta hai.

Agar same Key use karte hain to messages same Partition me jayenge aur order maintain rahega.

---

## Q125. Kafka me Load Balancing aur Fault Tolerance ek saath kaise achieve hote hain?

**Answer:**

* Partitions ko multiple Brokers me distribute karke **Load Balancing** achieve hota hai.
* Replication aur Leader Election ki wajah se **Fault Tolerance** achieve hota hai.

* Expected Traffic
* Number of Consumers
* Retention Requirement
* Scalability
* Naming Convention

### ⚠️ Ek important correction

Tumhare notes me ye line hai:

> **"Key = Message ka address"**

Interview me ise thoda aur technically sahi tarike se bolna:

> **"A Kafka message key is used by the producer's partitioner to determine the target partition. It also helps maintain ordering for messages with the same key."**

Ye answer interviewer ke saamne zyada professional aur technically accurate lagega.
